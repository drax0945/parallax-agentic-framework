# Macrocyclisation of ALK inhibitors cuts rotatable bonds and raises potency

In the crizotinib->lorlatinib series, closing a 12-membered macrolactam dropped rotatable bonds from 5 to 0 and improved ALK potency while retaining brain penetration.
