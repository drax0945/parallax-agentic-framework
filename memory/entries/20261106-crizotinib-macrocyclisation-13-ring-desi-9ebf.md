# Crizotinib macrocyclisation: 13-ring design removes 5 rotatable bonds

Design C[C@H]1Oc2cc(cnc2N)-c2cnn(c2)C2(CCNCC2)Cc2cc(F)c(Cl)c1c2Cl closes a 13-membered ring via CH2. Predicted pIC50 (biochemical inhibition) 8.38 with model chembl4247_inhibition (scaffold RMSE 0.657); domain verdict: outside domain — extrapolation, treat as a ranking hint only. Closure: C-C coupling or alkylation under high dilution; consider an intramolecular Suzuki. SA score 6.06 is the main makeability concern.
